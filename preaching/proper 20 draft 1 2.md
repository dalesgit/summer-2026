## Things are amiss
[[proper 20 draft 1]]

In ancient times a total solar eclipse was a sign from the universe (the gods?) that a mighty national empire might be overthrown. It could be a strong enough sign that whole populations might move from one place to another. 

Today it’s a photo-op. Or a missed photo-op as the case may be.

When I look around I see signs that things are amiss. For me it was more obvious as we traveled around the north Atlantic, following the path that Norse sailors had sailed a thousand years ago. 

If we have eyes to see and ears to hear ... But it appears more and more that we don’t have eyes and ears, because we have covered them up.

One of the things that going abroad does it affords the opportunity to look at one's home from a different perspective.

As for myself as I anticipated getting home to Palm Harbor things looked a mess. Some of you may see it differently. One of the things I’m glad to remember is is the time that I put such a question in a sermon in South Carolina. One old gentleman walked up to me after the service and said that he very much disagreed with me. I appreciated that deeply.
But I see things as having gone terribly awry in the world around me, and I don’t know what to do about it.
