## Reluctant prophets

Given the diagnosis, where do we turn? Who do we look to for guidance and direction? Given that we live in a society that is strongly influenced by a sense of “ain’t nobody gonna tell me what to do” Who or where might be fine the authority, the motivation to follow God‘s mission?

The old workhorses seem less and less capable. Theologies written for a world of centuries ago seem odd living in a world where all of our accumulated knowledge can multiply in days not centuries? The old authorities hold less and less for us. To whom do we turn?

Tired.
